隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

「Beepnote」（以下稱本 App）是一款以離線為主的個人記事工具。我們的原則很簡單：你的資料只存在你的手機上，我們看不到、也不會收集。

我們收集什麼資料

不收集任何資料。本 App 沒有帳號、沒有伺服器、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告，也沒有任何第三方 SDK。你輸入的作業、考試、行程、待辦、記帳與聊天訊息，全部只儲存在你裝置本機的資料庫中。

資料儲存在哪裡

所有資料儲存在你的 iPhone 上，由 iOS 的沙盒保護。如果你有開啟 iCloud 裝置備份，這些資料會跟著 iOS 備份一起備份到你自己的 iCloud 帳號；我們無法存取該備份。

通知

本 App 使用 iOS 的「本機通知」提醒作業截止、考試與行程。提醒是在你的裝置上排程的，不會經過任何伺服器。你可以隨時在 iOS 設定中關閉通知。

網路連線

只有「新聞」頁會連線到新聞媒體的公開 RSS（中央社、自由時報、鉅亨網、BBC 等）讀取標題，點開文章時由 app 內的 Safari 瀏覽該媒體網站；這些連線由你的裝置直接發出，本 App 不會在中間經手，也不會把你的任何資料傳給任何人。若你在「自訂簡報」裡打開天氣，App 會向 Open-Meteo（免帳號的公開天氣服務）查你所在地的預報，只送出經緯度，不會保存或上傳你的位置；定位權限可隨時在系統設定關閉，關閉後簡報只是少了天氣那行。其餘功能完全不連線。

如何刪除資料

在 App 內可以逐筆刪除。要刪除全部資料，直接從 iPhone 移除本 App 即可，所有資料會一併刪除。

兒童隱私

本 App 不收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

政策變更

若本政策有更新，會在本頁公告並更新生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Beepnote (the "App") is an offline-first personal note-taking tool. The principle is simple: your data stays on your phone. We cannot see it and we do not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. The App has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs. The homework, exams, events, to-dos, spending and chat messages you enter are stored only in a database on your device.

Where your data lives

All data is kept on your iPhone, protected by the iOS app sandbox. If you have iCloud device backup turned on, this data is included in your own iOS backup; we have no access to that backup.

Notifications

The App uses iOS local notifications to remind you of due dates, exams and events. Reminders are scheduled on your device and never pass through a server. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Network

Only the News page connects to the publishers' public RSS feeds (BBC, CBC, Taipei Times, Focus Taiwan, CNA and others) to read headlines, and opens articles in an in-app Safari view on the publisher's own site. Those connections go directly from your device; the App does not relay them and never sends any of your data to anyone. If you turn on weather in a custom brief, the App asks Open-Meteo (a public forecast service with no account) for your local forecast, sending only your coordinates; your location is never stored or uploaded, and you can withdraw location access in Settings at any time, which simply drops the weather line. Everything else works without a network connection.

Deleting your data

Items can be deleted one by one inside the App. To delete everything, remove the App from your iPhone; all of its data is deleted with it.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
